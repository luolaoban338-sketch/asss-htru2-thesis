Six selectors retain four variables; the all-eight arm provides context. Every arm uses the same L2 logistic regression and a probability threshold of 0.5. Selection uses training data only and is frozen before held-out values are loaded.

ASSS 111 uses B=4 contribution resamples, M=256 permutations, three utility folds, lambda=0.5 and eta=0.1. Mean Shapley 100 shares the contribution estimates; 000 is its structural alias. The root seed remains 2026101801, with fold-specific seed derivation preserved. The frozen grids, model specifications and tie rules are unchanged.
