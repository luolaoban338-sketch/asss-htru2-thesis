# Claim and evidence index / 主张与证据对照

The identifiers in the English manuscript and the Chinese reading copy refer to the same results. A1–A4 concern one real development dataset. B1–B5 concern prespecified synthetic settings. D1–D3 identify interpretations and questions for future work. Exact numerical fields are indexed in the A3 source map (source: `thesis_method_validation_20261018/synthesis_20261030/P3_20261102_delivered_20261101/editorial/A3_sources.json`) and the A4 source map (source: `thesis_method_validation_20261018/synthesis_20261030/P3_20261102_delivered_20261101/editorial/A4_sources.json`).
