# Selected evidence

`ablation_20261011/` contains byte-preserving copies of the fixed-budget aggregate snapshot and its archived independent aggregate-check report. Original source paths and hashes are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.

The snapshot covers 465 predictive records, 270 stability selections, 300 contribution games, 31 absolute summary rows and 135 primary seed-level F1 contrasts (all zero). The fixed-budget stability summaries are descriptive. They do not establish general robustness or absence of module effects.

The complete per-package model artifacts and fit ledger are retained in the original local project. The copied audit report describes the audit of that complete project, not a new full audit performed on this reduced sharing snapshot.
